## Observations and Gaps

* **Rapid strategy execution vs. cross‑market rotation** – Most platforms above focus on data aggregation, document synthesis or strategy backtesting. None explicitly detect or narrate leadership rotation across sectors and asset classes. TiMi and Trade Vector emphasise execution but not regime labeling.
* **Breadth and fragility** – Tools like Qlib and QuantConnect provide modelling infrastructure but do not surface breadth deterioration or narrow leadership warnings. Morningstar and LSEG offer broad data but limited rotation intelligence.
* **Causal explanation and narrative** – Platforms such as Hebbia and AlphaSense emphasise reasoning over documents but not dynamic regime labelling. Captide demonstrates structured agentic processing yet focuses on document extraction rather than market state transitions.
* **Time‑series change narration** – None of the reviewed systems automatically narrate what changed in leadership over different time horizons. Users must manually interpret charts and analytics.

These gaps highlight the opportunity for **RotationAtlas** to provide cross‑market rotation intelligence with sector‑to‑industry drill‑down, causal explanations (breadth, macro context) and change‑over‑time narration that current tools lack.

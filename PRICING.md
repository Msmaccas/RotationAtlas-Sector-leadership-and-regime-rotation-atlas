# RotationAtlas Pricing

RotationAtlas is a research tool under active development.  Pricing is illustrative and subject to change.  The free tier exists to encourage experimentation, while paid tiers add data integrations, extended history and support for larger teams.

| Tier | Monthly price | Features |
|----|---------------|---------|
| **Basic** | Free | Access to the open‑source core; uses sample fixture data; CLI & API access; daily rotation reports with 1‑month history; community support. |
| **Professional** | USD 49 | Includes all Basic features plus live data connectors for US sector ETFs and macro proxies; up to 5 concurrent users; dashboard access (when available); email reports and change alerts. |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Includes all Professional features plus global data coverage (international ETFs, industries and commodities); custom factor models via Python sidecar; on‑prem or private‑cloud deployment; single sign‑on (SSO); dedicated support and SLA. |

**Note:** RotationAtlas does not provide trading advice or guarantees of performance.  Pricing covers access to analytics and infrastructure.  Data vendor fees may apply and are passed through separately.
